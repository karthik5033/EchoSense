# Agent Instructions & Design Guidelines

## UI & Design Preferences
- **NO Gimmicky Badges or Overlay Pills**: NEVER add unrequested floating badges, status chips, pulsing ping indicators ("live" dots), fake metric pills (e.g. "10 MS/s Telemetry Stream • Seamless Loop"), or mode switcher bars on top of or around media, videos, charts, or UI components.
- **Clean Asset Presentation**: When asked to embed videos, images, or showcases, embed the raw, pristine media directly in its clean container. Do not add decorative flair, artificial badges, or redundant toggle controls unless explicitly requested by the user.
- **Minimalist Enterprise Aesthetics**: Keep UI clean, functional, and authentic. Avoid clutter and unsolicited marketing-style decorative elements.
